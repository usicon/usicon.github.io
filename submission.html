Submission

Paper Requirements

  1. Papers must be written in English.
  2. Papers must follow the author guidelines and template.
  3. All papers must be submitted through the USICON OJS.
  4. Participants must complete the designated Google Form at s.id/USICON-submissionform.
  5. The best papers will be selected for presentation and for publication in the proceedings of a reputable international publisher, subject to the publisher's editorial review and requirements, including additional fees where applicable.

Ready to submit?

Upload your full paper to the OJS, then fill in the registration form. Extended deadline: October 30, 2026.
