Keynote Speakers

Four scholars from Iran, the United States, the Netherlands, and Malaysia will deliver keynote lectures on the theme From Fasād to Iṣlāḥ: Islamic Thought and Ethics for a Regenerative Civilization.

Keynote Speaker

Prof. Dr. Mohammad Sharifani, Ph.D.

Allameh Tabataba'i University, Tehran, Iran · Director, Islamic Cultural Center (ICC) Jakarta

Mohammad Sharifani is Professor of Islamic Thought at Allameh Tabataba'i University, Tehran, and Director of the Islamic Cultural Center (ICC) in Jakarta. He trained in the Islamic sciences at the Ḥawzah ʿIlmiyyah of Qom and in Islamic philosophy at the Imam Khomeini Education and Research Institute, and earned his Ph.D. in Philosophy of Education from Shiraz University. His work spans thematic Qur'anic exegesis, the transcendent philosophy (al-ḥikmah al-mutaʿāliyah) of Mullā Ṣadrā, Islamic psychology and education, and Nahj al-Balāghah studies. He has written more than one hundred books and many scholarly articles, and has supervised numerous doctoral students.

  • Qur'anic exegesis
  • Islamic philosophy
  • Islamic education

Keynote Speaker

Assoc. Prof. Mark Woodward, Ph.D.

Arizona State University, United States of America

Mark Woodward is Associate Professor of Religious Studies at Arizona State University, where he has taught since 1985. An anthropologist by training (Ph.D., University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign), he has studied religion, politics, and identity in Indonesia and Southeast Asia for four decades, and has taught as a visiting professor at Universitas Gadjah Mada and UIN Sunan Kalijaga in Yogyakarta. His books include Islam in Java: Normative Piety and Mysticism in the Sultanate of Yogyakarta (1989), Java, Indonesia and Islam (2011), and, with Richard Martin and Dwi Atmaja, Defenders of Reason in Islam: Muʿtazilism from Medieval School to Modern Symbol (1997).

  • Anthropology of religion
  • Islam in Southeast Asia
  • Religion and politics

Keynote Speaker

Prof. F.J.S. Wijsen (Frans), Ph.D.

Radboud University, Nijmegen, Netherlands

Frans Wijsen is Professor Emeritus of Empirical and Practical Religious Studies at Radboud University, Nijmegen, and since 2022 an adjunct professor at the Graduate School of Universitas Gadjah Mada, where he teaches in the Indonesian Consortium for Religious Studies (ICRS) and the Center for Religious and Cross-cultural Studies (CRCS). His research covers interreligious relations, religion and conflict, and religion and nature, with long-term fieldwork in Tanzania and Indonesia. He co-directs a comparative Dutch–Indonesian project on humans and nature, is editor-in-chief of Studies in Interreligious Dialogue, and has supervised many Indonesian doctoral students.

  • Interreligious studies
  • Religion and nature
  • Religion and conflict

Keynote Speaker

Dr. Muhammad Arif Yahya

Universiti Kebangsaan Malaysia, Malaysia

Muhammad Arif Yahya is Senior Lecturer at the Centre for Usuluddin and Philosophy Studies, Faculty of Islamic Studies, Universiti Kebangsaan Malaysia (UKM), where he headed the undergraduate programme in Usuluddin and Philosophy from 2021 to 2023. A graduate of al-Azhar University, he earned his master's and Ph.D. in hadith studies from the Academy of Islamic Studies, Universiti Malaya. His research covers hadith sciences, Prophetic narratives (al-qaṣaṣ al-nabawī), hadith pedagogy, wasaṭiyyah in Nusantara Qur'anic exegesis, and youth intervention grounded in Prophetic psychology, including projects funded by Malaysia's Fundamental Research Grant Scheme (FRGS).

  • Hadith studies
  • Wasaṭiyyah
  • Prophetic psychology
